Relativistic Black Hole Ray-Marcher

This visualization renders a Kerr-inspired rotating black hole using real-time full-screen GPU ray-marching. Rather than drawing geometry, light paths (null geodesics) are numerical integrated and bent toward the singularity according to gravitational deflection approximations.

Key phenomena simulated include:

  • Gravitational Lensing: Light rays from behind the event horizon warp above and below the black hole, showing the far side of the accretion disk simultaneously.
  • Photon Ring: Rays executing multiple orbits around $r \approx 2.6 r_g$ create a razor-thin, bright ring at the shadow boundary.
  • Relativistic Doppler Boosting: Disk matter orbiting toward the observer is blueshifted and significantly brightened; receding matter is redshifted and dimmed.
  • ISCO Recession: Increasing the Kerr spin parameter ($a^*$) shrinks the Innermost Stable Circular Orbit, bringing hot plasma closer to the event horizon.
